Privacy

Effective November 10, 2026. Clickit operates from Thailand. Contact thammatorn.j@gmail.com for privacy or purchase support.

Answer requests

Hosted Text sends the question and choices through Clickit's Cloudflare service and OpenRouter to TypeSafe's Jev model. Hosted Vision sends a resized image of the whole visible tab, which may contain personal information. Vision captures only when you press Answer or its hotkey; check the tab first.

Clickit processes this content in memory, does not save it in its database or intentionally log it. Chrome temporarily retains the latest suggestion per tab. OpenRouter and TypeSafe process requests under their OpenRouter and TypeSafe privacy policies and TypeSafe's customer agreements. Provider-side zero retention is not guaranteed.

Records and billing

We store a random installation ID, hashed access key, acquisition source, timestamps, credit balances, request status, mode, token usage when supplied, latency and estimated cost. We store a keyed hash of the registration IP to limit repeat trials, not the raw IP in our database. Cloudflare receives network information under its privacy terms.

Chrome stores your access key and settings unencrypted in extension storage, accessible to trusted extension pages, not quiz pages. Keep your backup key private; uninstalling may delete the local key.

Stripe handles payment information. We store customer, subscription, checkout and invoice references; plan and billing periods; amounts, available tax and fees, refunds and the associated installation. Pending billing operations retain retry details. Stripe may collect your email and billing address; Clickit does not receive or store card numbers.

Your own API key

Advanced API-key mode sends requests directly to your selected TypeSafe or OpenRouter service, bypassing Clickit's inference service and credits. The provider receives the question or screenshot and bills you. Your key stays in trusted extension storage and is not sent to quiz pages.

Use and retention

Clickit follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including Limited Use. We use data to provide requested suggestions, maintain access and billing, prevent abuse and provide support. We do not sell data or use quiz content for advertising. Humans access quiz content only with your specific consent or when required for security or legal compliance.

Technical request and session records are kept up to 90 days. Installation and credit records remain as needed for access. Billing records remain as required for accounting, access, retries and disputes. Records also support recovery, support, fraud prevention and service measurement.

Email us to request access, correction or deletion. We verify purchase ownership before changing purchase access. Deletion may end access to credits; we will explain any records we must retain.

Audience and transfers

Clickit is for adults 18 and older. Cloudflare, OpenRouter, TypeSafe and Stripe may process data outside your country. Share only information you have permission to share.
