Privacy

Effective November 7, 2026. Contact thammatorn.j@gmail.com for privacy or purchase support. ClickIt is operated from Thailand.

What an answer request sends

In hosted Text mode, the extension sends the detected question and answer choices through ClickIt's Cloudflare-hosted service and OpenRouter to TypeSafe's Jev model. In hosted Vision mode, it sends a resized PNG of the whole visible tab through the same service. A screenshot may contain names, messages, or other personal information. Vision is disabled in hosted mode until validation passes.

ClickIt processes quiz content and screenshots in memory, does not store them in its database, and does not intentionally log them. Your browser temporarily retains the latest suggestion per tab. OpenRouter processes requests under its privacy policy and forwards them to TypeSafe under its privacy policy and customer agreements. ClickIt does not promise provider-side zero retention.

Installation and billing records

We store a random installation ID, a hash of its access key, acquisition source, timestamps, credit balances, request status, mode, token usage when supplied, latency, and estimated inference cost. We store a keyed hash of the registration IP address to limit repeat trials, not the raw IP address in the application database. Cloudflare receives network information when serving requests under its own privacy terms.

Your installation access key and settings are stored unencrypted in Chrome's extension storage. Trusted extension pages can access them; page scripts are not given these credentials. Keep your backup key private. Deleting the extension can delete its local access key.

Stripe handles payment information. ClickIt stores checkout and payment references, purchase amount, tax and fee information when available, refund totals, and the installation associated with each purchase. Stripe may collect your email and billing address. ClickIt does not receive or store card numbers.

Your own API key

In the advanced API-key mode, requests go directly to the selected TypeSafe or OpenRouter service, bypassing ClickIt's inference service and credit system. The selected provider receives the question or screenshot and bills your account. Your key stays in trusted extension storage and is not sent to quiz pages.

Use and retention

Records support credit fulfillment, recovery, support, fraud prevention, service measurement, and bookkeeping. We do not sell personal data or use quiz content for advertising. Technical request and session records are retained for up to 90 days. Installation and credit records remain while needed to provide access. Purchase and refund records remain for applicable accounting and dispute obligations.

Email us to request access, correction, or deletion. We verify ownership before changing purchase access. Deletion can end access to remaining credits; we explain any records we must retain before completing a request.

Audience and transfers

The pilot is for adults aged 18 or older. Cloudflare, OpenRouter, TypeSafe, and Stripe may process information outside your country. Do not send information you do not have permission to share.
