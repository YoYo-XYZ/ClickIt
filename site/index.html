BEFORE YOU TRY

Supported pages matter.

ClickIt needs one visible single-choice question and readable choices. It does not answer typed or multi-select questions, submit quizzes, or follow questions automatically.

When a participant page shows only colored shapes, the question on another screen is missing. ClickIt cannot answer from that screenshot. Live quiz compatibility is being validated. We do not claim support for every Kahoot session or other quiz platform.

Vision is experimental and remains disabled in hosted mode until validation passes. Accuracy and response time vary. Use ClickIt for casual quizzes where assistance is permitted.
