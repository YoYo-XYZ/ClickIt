The service

Clickit suggests one answer to a visible single-choice question when you press Answer. It selects a supported control or highlights a region; it never submits or advances. Answers can be wrong. Compatibility and speed vary. The pilot is for adults 18 and older; use it for casual quizzes where assistance is permitted and follow platform rules.

Trial and subscriptions

Eligible new installations receive 15 free suggestions without a card, subject to capacity and abuse limits. Basic costs US$4.99 per month for 500 credits; Plus costs US$9.99 per month for 1,200. Checkout shows your total. Plans renew monthly until canceled, on the date shown by Stripe. There are no new one-time top-ups.

A valid suggestion costs one credit, even if wrong, highlighted for manual selection or already selected. Failed requests, visibility failures, invalid responses and page-change rejections cost nothing. Monthly credits expire each billing period and do not roll over. Failed payments grant no new monthly credits until paid. Free and previously purchased one-time credits retain their original terms and have no scheduled expiry while Clickit operates.

Changes and cancellation

Upgrading to Plus charges the prorated difference after you confirm the quote. Payment adds 700 credits until the existing renewal date without resetting used credits. Plus then renews at US$9.99 monthly. Downgrades start at renewal.

Cancel renewal in the extension or Manage subscription. Remaining monthly credits last until the paid period ends; you may undo cancellation before then. Back up your access key to manage billing after reinstalling.

Refunds

Subscriptions are non-refundable except for billing errors or where law requires. Cancel before renewal to avoid the next charge. Mandatory consumer rights still apply. Contact support privately with your purchase email and receipt reference. Refunds revoke only credits associated with the refunded payment.

Previous one-time packs retain their purchase terms, including any first-pack seven-day guarantee. If Clickit closes, we will give notice, stop renewals, honor applicable refund rights and refund unused legacy purchased credits as promised.

Provider outages or pilot spending limits may pause answers; they do not extend billing periods or credit expiry.

Access and support

Privately back up the access key in Advanced settings. Anyone with it can use your credits. If lost, email support, preferably from your purchase email. We verify payment ownership before rotating access and aim to reply within one business day.

Do not abuse trials, bypass metering, share access publicly or harm others. We may pause abusive access and will review unused paid balances and applicable refund rights when resolving a suspension.
